Shazia Aftab

Chief Executive Officer

Shazia Aftab leads Nexus Property as CEO, bringing a wealth of experience and a dynamic vision to the company. She has been instrumental in shaping Nexus Property’s strategy, culture, and growth, ensuring the company remains a leader in the real estate sector of Delhi NCR and beyond. Her leadership is defined by a commitment to innovation, transparency, and ethical business practices. Shazia Aftab is passionate about empowering her team, fostering a collaborative environment, and delivering exceptional value to every client and partner. Under her guidance, Nexus Property has expanded its portfolio of residential and commercial projects, consistently exceeding client expectations and setting new benchmarks for quality and service. Shazia Aftab’s focus on customer satisfaction and long-term relationships has earned the trust of hundreds of clients. Beyond her professional achievements, Shazia Aftab is dedicated to community development and social responsibility. She believes in making a positive impact through ethical leadership, continuous learning, and a relentless pursuit of excellence in every aspect of the business.
